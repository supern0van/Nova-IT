# Nova IT identity direction

## Selected direction

**Signal N** is the working logo direction for Nova IT. The mark combines a
compact N with a small signal point. It should communicate a clear response,
direction and dependable support without relying on the usual IT symbols.

The first exploration compared six directions. Signal N was selected because
it has the strongest silhouette and can be recognised without the wordmark.
The compact sixth direction remains a useful small-size reference, but is not
the primary identity direction.

## Current implementation

- `public/nova-it-mark.svg` is the primary dark application for the website
  header and browser icon.
- `public/nova-it-mark-inverse.svg` is the light application for dark surfaces.
- The current mark is a clean working vector, suitable for testing on the site.

## Preserve

- Deep ink blue with a restrained signal-blue point.
- A simple, compact N silhouette with clear negative space.
- One-colour legibility at favicon size.
- A calm, capable service tone rather than a consumer-repair or startup look.

## Avoid

- Shields, locks, gears, Wi-Fi arcs, cloud symbols, robots and circuit motifs.
- Neon palettes, gradients, gaming aesthetics and decorative space imagery.
- Treating generated typography as final brand artwork.

## Before external production

The working mark needs a trademark and availability check before the company
is registered, printed or used as a final identity. A production designer should
also make the final vector master, wordmark, spacing rules, monochrome variants
and a small-size favicon test.
